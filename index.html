FILEDROP CAPABILITIES

Chunk Processing

To make resume support possible at any point the transaction, i use chunk tracking and log successful transfers and resume transfer from incomplete ones.

Background Service

Receiver always running as a service in the background always accepting requests without opening an app connection is made automatically, and spawns a request ui to start transfer(s).

⚡

Fast Speed

Quick transfers possible through multi-threading and async, to achieve quick transfer speeds even across platforms.
